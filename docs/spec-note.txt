Spec note

Sickle(Melee)
Button budget: 6 rotation buttons + 1 mobility skill
Range: 1 tile, diagonals included. Distances are tiles now - meters are gone

0. auto-attack: Hits the primary target only, within range 1. Generates no Rage. Attack speed is a property of the weapon, hits per second, not a stat gear rolls. It costs no button and no GCD - it just runs
1. builder(ST) - Furious Thrust: Brutally stabs the primary target. Generates 1c of Rage
2. builder(AoE) - Brutal Swing: Cuts every enemy three wide and two rows ahead, based on facing. The only sickle skill that reaches past range 1, and the only one where facing matters. Generates 0.5c of Rage per target hit. Caps at 5 targets
3. spender(ST) - Overhead Slam: Slams the primary target for heavy damage. Damage scales with combo points spent on a smooth curve - no sharp jump between steps, so holding for 5c should not always be the right call. Exact numbers TBD
4. spender(AoE) - Whirling Slash: Channeled. Spins in place, cutting every enemy in range 1. 0.7s duration per combo point, movement allowed while channeling, ticking damage. Ticks are re-checked every tick against whoever is standing in range 1 right then, so walking away wastes ticks and walking back resumes them - no separate rule needed. Also applies a bleed, but only to the primary target: with no target selected the ticks still land and the bleed does not. Cannot be interrupted once started, and restores a flat amount of health on completion - amount TBD, has to stay well under the healing potion's 60%. The completion heal only lands if at least one tick connected
5. main cd - Market Price Rage: Enraged by crop inflation. All attack power increased by 20%, 1m cd, 15s sustain
6. mini cd - Tilling: Tears a rift in the ground covering range 1 around the player. Every enemy in the rift takes 10% increased damage. 30s cd, 15s sustain
7. mobility - Sprint: Movement speed increased by 30%. Lasts 10 seconds, 20s cd

Notes
- Brutal Swing caps at 5 targets, which settles the old worry that a big enough pack would fill all 5 combo points in one swing. 5 targets is 2.5c, so it always takes two swings. The cap lives on the base skill, not on the talent that widens the area
- Brutal Swing is three wide, not two, because an even width has no centre tile for the player to stand on. Two wide would need a rule about which side it favours, and would hit the monster on one flank but not the other
- Auto-attacks generating no Rage is what finally kills haste as an equipment stat. A faster auto-attack that feeds nothing cannot change the rotation, and a talent that only changes numbers is arithmetic
- Tier 2a spreads the bleed from the primary target, so with no target selected there is nothing to spread. Targeting is not optional for that build
- Spreading the bleed to nearby enemies per Whirling Slash tick is now a confirmed talent (tier 2, option A). Because the bleed rides on the AoE spender, Whirling Slash has a reason to be pressed in single target too
- Whirling Slash cannot be interrupted. The cost is not a rule so much as a loss: quit early and the completion heal never lands. Movement stays free during the channel - being locked in for 3.5s with no movement as well would be too much. The real price is opportunity cost, since the GCD is fixed and a 5c channel is two or more skills not pressed
- Rage is stored internally as integer units (1c = 10 units) so the 0.5c generation never needs a float
- Spenders consume whole combo points only. At 2.5c a spender uses 2c and the leftover half stays on the bar for the next builder to top up
- Brutal Swing on 5 targets generates 2.5c, so it beats Furious Thrust from 2 targets up and ties at exactly 2


Talent tree
5 tiers, one pick per tier, 2 points each - exactly the 10 points available. 13 talents total
Names are TBD. Effects only for now
Rule used while picking these: a talent has to change the rotation. A flat damage increase is arithmetic, not a choice, and arithmetic has one right answer

Tier 1 - builders
a. Furious Thrust damage up sharply
b. Brutal Swing area 2x3 -> 3x3, plus a damage increase. The 5-target cap stays, so maximum Rage does not change - what changes is how easy it is to actually reach 5

Tier 2 - bleed
a. Whirling Slash spreads the bleed to 2 nearby targets per tick
b. Bleed duration and damage up

Tier 3 - spenders
a. Overhead Slam used at 5c refunds 2c
b. Damage taken reduced while channeling Whirling Slash
c. The builder used right after a spender generates double Rage

Tier 4 - cooldowns
a. Spenders cost fewer combo points while Market Price Rage is up
b. Brutal Swing generates double Rage while standing inside Tilling's rift
c. Using both cooldowns at the same time adds an extra effect

Tier 5 - finishers
a. Spenders used at 5c always crit
b. Spenders deal increased damage to bleeding targets
c. After Sprint, the next builder - either one - instantly generates 5c

Notes on the tree
- Tier 4 was rewritten from scratch. The first draft was "cooldown lasts longer" and "cooldown comes back sooner", which is exactly the arithmetic the rule above rejects
- Tier 3b replaced "keep move speed while channeling". That talent assumed a movement penalty during the channel, which was never in the spec - it came from WoW habit, not from this design
- Tier 4b is the only talent in the tree that makes position matter, which is worth having in a grid-based world
- Unresolved: tier 3a and tier 5a both reward holding to 5c, which pushes against Overhead Slam's deliberately smooth curve. Intended synergy, or move one?
- Tier 5c is worth more in single target (+4c) than in AoE (+2.5c), which props up the branch where Rage builds slowest. It also turns Sprint from a movement skill into part of the damage rotation
- Tier 2a's real strength depends on the tick interval, which is still undecided
